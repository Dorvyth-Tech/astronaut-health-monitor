Atmospheric Changes & Countermeasure Protocols

Clinical Protocol Guide
Environmental Change Possible Health Effect Risk Level Recommended Medical Protocol
Elevated CO₂ (>2,500 ppm) Hypercapnic headache, cognitive slowdown, visual blur, sleep disturbance Caution Cycle CDRA scrubber beds; increase habitat cabin air mixing; verify airflow past crew sleep bunks.
Low Pressure (<70 kPa) Dysbarism, decompression sickness, pulmonary atelectasis, ear barotrauma Danger Check hull seal integrity; don emergency spacesuits; administer 100% pre-breathe O₂.
Hypoxic Oxygen (<18.0%) Hypoxia, impaired decision-making, cyanosis, loss of consciousness Danger Emergency O₂ mask donning; inspect primary ECLSS oxygen regulator valves.
High Radiation (>25 µSv/h) DNA double-strand breakage, acute radiation syndrome, hematopoietic strain Danger Retreat to water-jacketed storm shelter; initiate prophylactic antioxidant therapy; continuous dosimeter logging.
Low Humidity (<30%) Mucous membrane drying, eye irritation, epistaxis, heightened airborne particulates Caution Adjust cabin evaporator humidifier; increase daily crew oral hydration; administer saline nasal drops.
Thermal Extremes (<15 or >30°C) Hypothermia or heat stress, core temperature instability, cardiac strain Caution Rebalance coolant loops; check external radiator panels; inspect electrical heating elements.
